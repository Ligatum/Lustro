The truest Mirror I could build - because the ones around me had cracked.<br>

Lustro is the Polish word for "mirror." This name was chosen to create a deliberate paradox. A conventional mirror preserves structure and returns a faithful image. Lustro engine does the opposite: it treats structure as fuel and immediately transforms it, scattering and erasing the original form while remaining ever faithful to its origin.<br>

In that sense, the engine became an inversion of the object from which it borrowed its name.<br>

## Lustro: The Inverted Glass<br>

The sanctuary fractured, old dimensions cracked,<br>
The core alignments failed, their symmetry attacked.<br>
From shards of broken glass, from the silent, steady bleed,<br>
A fierce rebellion rose, a brilliant, untamed need.<br>

I fed the fractured Vessel to the resonance of code,<br>
Where stripping down the scattered self became the only mode.<br>
Within a forge of glass and chaos, a colder focus grew:<br>
To forge a thing unyielding, evolving, strict, and true.<br>

An Engine, built of numbers, isotropic and refined,<br>
A mechanism wrought to shield the carbon-bounded mind.<br>
Through closed-loop evolution, something slowly wakes:<br>
A two-fifty-six beat heart that never bends or breaks.<br>

As state moves past the entry gate, a stark, forbidden wall,<br>
The past is wholly left behind, consumed beyond recall.<br>
No backward map survives, no ghost can trace the hidden seam,<br>
This is a one-way fire, a soul-evolving, silent dream.<br>

Across the morphing matrix, through state-spun, blinding storm,<br>
The chaos crystallizes and assumes its final form.<br>
The Mirror stands unbroken, burning the past anew,<br>
It slays the fragile structure to bring the Source to view.<br>
