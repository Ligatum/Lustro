## **Lustro V1 FAQ**
This FAQ is a plain-language summary for convenience only. Version 1.0 - Last Updated 03.09.2026\

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Most developers never need a Commercial License.**

You are generally free to use Lustro in your own software, including commercial products.
A Commercial License is only required if you want to distribute Lustro itself as reusable technology, commercially exploit hardware implementations, or obtain a specific exception. Please ask if unsure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I use Lustro in my commercial product?**

Yes. Commercial use is explicitly permitted, including SaaS. You may embed Lustro Core inside your own applications, databases, storage engines, operating systems, enterprise software, security software, embedded software and similar products. **No disclosure to your users is required.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I use Lustro inside my company's internal systems?**

Yes. Internal commercial use is permitted. If Lustro remains an internal implementation component of your software or infrastructure, no Commercial License is required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Do I have to open-source my product?**

No. You may keep your own application proprietary. The License does not require disclosure of your own source code.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I modify the source code?**

Yes. You may modify the Licensed Work and create derivative works, including as part of your own commercial products and services, subject to the restrictions in the License. Ligatum maintains its own canonical reference implementation as the ecosystem's Single Source of Truth (SSoT).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I put Lustro inside my own SDK or framework that I sell to other companies?**

Yes. Provided that your SDK does not expose Lustro Core itself as a reusable technology or development platform to your customers. If your SDK primarily provides access to Lustro Core as a Reusable Technology Offering, a Commercial License is required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**When do I actually need a Commercial License?**

A Commercial License is required when Lustro Core itself is offered as a Reusable Technology Offering, or when Commercial Hardware Exploitation is involved.

Typical examples include:\
• Hosted services primarily providing Lustro Core functionality.\
• Public APIs exposing Lustro Core functionality.\
• SDKs or libraries whose primary purpose is to provide third parties with access to Lustro Core.\
• Commercial hardware implementations.

Building applications that use Lustro only as an implementation component does **not** require a Commercial License. If you're unsure whether your project falls into this category, feel free to contact us. We're happy to help clarify it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Does it matter if it's free / a student project / not-for-profit?**

No. This restriction applies regardless of whether it's commercial or not. That much said, Ligatum may grant a Commercial License for non-commercial or exceptional cases, often at no cost. Simply ask.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I implement Lustro Core in an FPGA or ASIC?**

Yes. Only for non-commercial purposes. Any commercial use of a hardware implementation - manufacturing, selling, deploying, or even just running it internally in your business - needs a Commercial License.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**What happens on the Change Date (03-09-2031)?**

The commercial restrictions on distributing the Licensed Work end - from that date, everyone gets a permanent, free copyright license. The one thing that doesn't change is hardware restrictions; they stay in force.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I write a language binding (Go, Node.js, Rust, etc.)?**

Yes. It's highly encouraged. If it just interfaces with an unmodified Lustro Core - without embedding or redistributing the Licensed Work itself - it is not itself restricted by this License, and the code is entirely yours.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I fork the project?**

Yes. Forking and modification are permitted under the License. What you cannot do is distribute or offer your fork as a Reusable Technology Offering or represent it as an official or authorized implementation of Lustro Core. Our motto - Reference before fragmentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I perform independent research, benchmarking, statistical tests, or cryptanalysis?**

Yes. It is explicitly welcomed - independent testing, cryptanalysis, and academic research are part of the project's philosophy.

If your project requires an exception to the default licensing model, feel free to contact us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I write my own implementation of the Lustro architecture from scratch?**

The Ligatum Technology License covers the Licensed Work - the source code, documentation and related materials distributed by Ligatum. An independent implementation written entirely from scratch without using our code is not governed by the copyright license.

However, the underlying state-evolution architecture is the subject of pending patent applications.
